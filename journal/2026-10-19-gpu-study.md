# A second concept: GPU-S1

Owner feedback on the vacuum was “this is quite good.” They asked for another
product and whether the framework is extensible, then selected GPU over the
suggested pressure cooker. The vacuum artifact is preserved. This is a new local
candidate, not acceptance of a GPU lesson or a universal provider/engine selection.

## Decision and hypothesis

Reuse the successful material finish and object-first interaction. For computation,
use a declared logical working view rather than fictitious physical machinery.
The hypothesis/rubric in `experiments/gpu-study/BRIEF.md` asks whether a beginner
can distinguish independent jobs from a prerequisite chain and transfer to two
parallel chains. No beginners have been tested; learning remains unmeasured.

The production gateway/provider/task routing, ConfigLoader, logger, Store and
atomic accounting already support reuse. The successful vacuum HTML still has
bespoke presentation code. Extracted `experiments/visual-studio` is a typed local
player/build shell; GPU is its first consumer. It owns controls, manual/tour
playback, seeking/reset, accessible labels, viewport framing and assessments.
GPU supplies a pure scheduler, teaching data and a reviewed drawing adapter.
The old vacuum player has not secretly been migrated or reapproved.
