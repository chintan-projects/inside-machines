# OpenAI + Anatomy adaptation

Apply common.md and the actual vendored Anatomy kit. Design a recognizable canister
cleaner with its working side opened, grounded wheels, a corrugated hose, a real
nozzle opening, a porous bag and a connected motor/fan/outlet assembly. Use filled
solids, four-tone surfaces, purposeful fittings, thickness and actual joints.
Do not build a pile of floating slabs or replace a cleaner with laboratory furniture.
Use kit geometry and projection. Keep unclipped open paths and correct visible depth.

The project explicitly overrides Anatomy's no-label rule: anchor readable core labels.
Air and dirt have semantic shapes/colors; labels and arrows must preserve meaning
without color. Stage focus can reveal a closeup, but always keep surrounding passage
walls and the shaft/support that explain the working part. End on the whole machine.
Only claim a geometry audit if that exact audit has run on the actual recorded geometry.
