# OpenAI + Remotion adaptation

Apply common.md using a real Remotion React composition and renderMedia output.
Treat each shot as an editorial decision: establish the machine, focus on the
relationship being spoken, preserve a simple locator, and reconnect the full path.
Use frame-driven positions and transforms so seeking and replay are deterministic.

Aim for a carefully illustrated field-guide film, not a marketing template: no
stock footage, gradient blobs, arbitrary kinetic words or staged title cards that
consume the explanation. Make labels feel attached to the object. Physical detail
must help someone understand where the air goes. Keep pressure qualitative and all
meaning aligned with the other candidates. Preserve a useful sound-off experience.
The shared browser chrome is not evidence that Remotion itself offers interactive
mechanism editing; distinguish exported film from the experiment UI.
