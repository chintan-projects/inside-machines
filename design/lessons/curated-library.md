# Curated library and shared studio adoption

Person: a curious family member asking how a familiar system works.
Job: open a clear visual explanation, explore a useful variation, or request a
missing topic and find its availability update on returning.
Posture: inviting, precise, composed.

Use the D1 studio's dominant visual, attached narration and adjacent controls;
use the existing library's actual titles/categories and private member sessions.
Avoid a wall of equal promotional cards or a request button that secretly spends
AI budget. Reuse D1 typography, blue primary action, cool stage, 8px controls and
14px viewer. Keep mechanism colors distinct from interface colors.

Tenets: a request is an expression of interest, not a paid job; one player serves
every current system; show a complete core before optional detail; sources and
model limits stay available; a draft never presents itself as human approved.

Twenty scopes: gasoline car (electric vehicles remain outside its scope), refrigerator, microwave,
washer, dishwasher, air conditioner, pressure cooker, induction cooktop, oven,
kettle, espresso machine, vacuum, table fan, water heater, toilet, sink trap,
faucet/water pressure, clothes dryer, heat pump and GPU. These are generic teaching
models, not service procedures or exact-model internals.

Notifications start in-app. Members see only their own requests and updates.
Owner triage, explicit budgeted build, review and release are separate actions.
Availability notices follow a released artifact and are idempotent per member.
No email delivery or browser notification permission is implied.

Hypothesis: after one core lesson, four of five initially unfamiliar learners
trace its input, working change and useful output and answer the lesson's changed
situation without hints. Each lesson retains its own claim/transfer record.
Test library → lesson → sound/experiment → return; missing request → owner queue →
review/release → requester's update; and owner cost disclosure. Review at
375/768/1440 in three bounded rounds. Engineering, owner taste, independent truth
and beginner evidence remain separate.
