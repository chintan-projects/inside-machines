## Mechanism and claim ledger

Electrical energy → motor/shaft → fan action → air movement. Room air enters the
nozzle/hose, crosses a porous collection bag and exits; exact component order and
bypasses vary. Teach these relationships together during normal running. No
numerical pressure/flow/particle counts, thermal model or exact internals are claimed.
Markers indicate air or retained dirt, with text and direction as well as color.
